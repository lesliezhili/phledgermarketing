# Content calendar

## Volume by plan

Take it from the package (`packages` in `src/lib/catalog.ts`): Grace 8 posts,
Faithful 12 posts + 12 short videos, Abundant as Faithful plus creator
content. Pay-per-lead clients: 3 posts a week, each ending in a way to enquire.

## Mix (per 10 posts)

- 4 **product or service** (what it is, the price, how to get it)
- 2 **behind the scenes** (making it, the team, the place)
- 2 **customer proof** (real reviews or photos shared with permission)
- 1 **seasonal or event** tied to the client's calendar
- 1 **community or faith** value post, only if it fits the client's voice

## Each post

```
Date / channel / format (photo, carousel, Reel/short, text)
Visual: <which client photo or clip, and the edit>
Caption: hook line (≤ 10 words) / 1–3 short lines of detail / call to action
Hashtags or tags: 3–8, local first (suburb, city), then topic
Link or QR: the tracked order or enquiry page
```

## Rules

- Use the client's real photos and numbers only. No stock photos of food or
  people pretending to be the client's.
- Every post that sells has one call to action and the tracked link.
- Write in the client's voice (profile: 3 words) and language.
- LinkedIn posts speak to businesses (corporate orders, team events); keep
  Instagram and TikTok visual and short.
- Draft only. Zhi Li approves on Monday; then they are scheduled.
