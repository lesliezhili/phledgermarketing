# Offer and print

Ads and posts are wasted until the offer, the price and the way to buy are
clear. This skill fixes that first.

## 1. The core offer

Write one line the whole campaign repeats, built from the client profile:

`<what they get> + <why it's better or different> + <price or "from $X"> + <how to get it>`

Example (café with cakes and a function room):
"Your celebration, sorted: book the room and your whole cake comes with it. Packages from $X pp. Enquire with the QR code."

Prefer a bundle that links two offers (venue + cake, service + aftercare).
Never invent a price or discount: ask, or write `[price to confirm]`.

## 2. Each print piece

Rewrite the copy in this order, then hand a brief to the designer (Canva template):

1. Business name and logo (top)
2. Headline: the offer in 3–7 words
3. 2–4 specifics: sizes, capacity, packages, prices, lead times
4. One call to action + QR code to the order or enquiry page
5. Address, phone, website or social handle (bottom)
6. A line pointing to the related offer ("Celebrating? Host it here too")

Checks before sign-off: spelling of every product name, prices match the price
list, the QR code goes to a tracked page, it reads at arm's length, no free
mailbox as the only contact.

## Output

The core offer line, the copy for each piece in the order above, and a short
designer brief (size, colours from the brand, photo to use).
