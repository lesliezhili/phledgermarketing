# Loyalty emails (keep customers coming back)

Repeat customers cost almost nothing to reach. Five automated messages cover
most businesses; write them once, schedule them, refresh seasonally.

| Message | Sent | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Thank you | Day after a purchase | Thanks, care tip or what to expect, ask for a Google review (link) |
| Birthday | 1 month before their birthday | A modest gift or discount the owner approves, how to book or order |
| Win-back | 60–90 days without a purchase (set per business) | What's new, a reason to return, no guilt |
| Seasonal | Before each busy period in the client's calendar | The seasonal offer, order-by date |
| Referral | After a second purchase | Invite them to bring a friend; both get the owner's chosen reward |

## Each message

Subject (≤ 45 characters), preview line, 3–5 short lines, one button to the
tracked order page, the business's name, address and an unsubscribe link.
Plain, warm, in the client's voice. Personalise with first name only.

## Rules

- Only email people who agreed to hear from the business (collected in store
  or online, with a clear tick box). Always include unsubscribe.
- Offers and discounts must be approved by the owner; never invent them.
- Measure: opens, clicks, and orders that used the email's link or code.
