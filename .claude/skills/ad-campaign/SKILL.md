# Ad campaign

Ad spend is paid by the client, at cost. Our job is to turn it into leads and
sales for less than the client's break-even.

## Plan

1. **Goal:** leads (enquiries and calls) unless the client sells online, then purchases.
2. **Platform** from the business type: local and personal services → Meta +
   Google Search; online products → Meta; B2B → LinkedIn + Google Search.
3. **Audience:** location radius from the client's service area; interests or
   job titles that match today's best customers; retarget site visitors.
4. **Budget:** the client's figure. Target cost per lead =
   (average sale × margin × expected close rate) ÷ 2, so ads stay clearly profitable.
5. **Tracking:** every ad goes to the tracked order/enquiry page or tracked
   number, so each lead is logged (see `lead-qualification`).

## Write 3 ad variants

Each: hook (≤ 40 characters), body (≤ 125 characters) with the offer and
price, call to action, the client's real photo or 6–15 second clip.

## Weekly optimisation

| Signal | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Cost per lead above target for 7 days | Pause the weakest variant; test a new hook |
| A variant at half the target cost | Move budget to it |
| Clicks but no enquiries | Fix the landing page before spending more |
| Pay-per-lead client near their cap | Lower spend; leads above the cap aren't paid |

Report spend, leads, cost per lead and sales against break-even in the
monthly report. Never use misleading claims, fake urgency or before/after
promises the client can't prove.
