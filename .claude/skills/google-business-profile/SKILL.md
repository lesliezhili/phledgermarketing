# Google Business Profile and local search

For local shops, venues and personal services this is usually the channel
that brings the most enquiries and calls, and it is free.

## Setup checklist

- Primary category is the main thing they sell; up to 4 secondary categories
- Description (≤ 750 characters): offer, area, what makes them different, how to order
- Hours, holiday hours, phone (tracked number for pay-per-lead clients), website (tracked page)
- Services and products listed with prices
- 10+ real photos: front of shop, inside, products, team; add new ones monthly
- Booking or order link, messaging switched on if the owner will answer
- A review-request card or QR code at the counter

## Weekly

- 1 Google post: offer, event or new product, with photo and button
- Reply to every new review within 48 hours (drafts for approval):
  - Positive: thank them by name, mention what they liked, invite them back
  - Negative: thank them, apologise for the specific problem, offer to fix it
    offline with a contact; never argue, never reveal private details
- Note views, calls, direction requests and website clicks for the report

## Rules

Never write or buy fake reviews, never offer rewards for positive reviews
(ask all customers equally), and never stuff keywords into the business name.
